    Grounding rituals

    I spend 10 unbroken minutes on one, then make a Constitution check against the DC above. Success raises my control by 1, failure drops it by 1, a natural 20 or 1 shifts it by 2.

    • Exhaust the body
    • Endure pain
    • Break something apart
    • Drill until it's rote
    • Go completely still
    • Haul something heavy
    • Stand against the elements
    • Bleed off the pressure

    These are just examples — flavor the scene at the table however it fits the moment.

    What sets me off

    When something triggers me, I make a Wisdom save or lose a level of control.

    • Someone I trust falls beside me.
    • My kin, my home, or my name gets mocked or threatened.
    • I'm forced to move or act against my own will.
    • Something drags me back to the table I was made on.
    • A word, a sound, or a mark reaches something buried in me.
    • I'm caged, cornered, pinned, or held down.

      The Reckoning

      Sealed until conditioning reaches the threshold. Then he chooses what he becomes.

      Rage Incarnate

      I give in

      The experiment wins. The one made on that operating table is all which remains. I am not Morrbark. I am the shape of rage, and there's no man left underneath to talk down.

      Rage is permanently active: +20 rage damage, +1 extra attack, immune to being charmed or frightened, and the first time each turn I drop an enemy to 0 HP I can move up to half my speed and make one melee attack. This replaces the Broken Seal penalties, but my control locks at 0, I can never ground again, and those who knew me feel they're speaking to a monster. There is no way back.

      Iron Will

      I seal it away

      I forge a will that nothing reaches, and the conditioning dies in me. I command the fury — it never again commands me.

      Can no longer rage: resistance to all damage types, as long as you have at least 1hp regenerate 5hp at the start of your turns, advantage on all attack rolls made using Strength. My control locks at Iron Discipline and can't fall below it; I keep its benefits. Once per day I can dig deep within me to unleash my rage on purpose: become Rage Incarnate for 1 round before reining it back in.

        How this works
        1. Grounding raises my control. A 10-minute ritual → Constitution check vs the shown DC. +1 on a hit, −1 on a miss, ±2 on a nat 20 or 1. Once per rest is a clean default.
        2. Triggers lower it. When something sets me off, I make a Wisdom save or the number drops. My tier tilts those saves.
        3. The tier changes my rage itself — shown live above, tagged for where it lands: in a fight, out of combat, or as pure roleplay texture (no dice). It persists between sessions.
        4. The sealed panel is the DM's — the word keeps the conditioning arc out of sight until it's time. Default word: elderwood. Change it inside.
